## Dog Person

I adopted my dog, Oli, in 2023 through [<u>Toby's Rescue</u>](https://www.tobysrescue.com/). She was found on the streets of Puerto Rico, rescued by local volunteers, and brought to the DMV area, where I was lucky enough to meet her. Inspired by Oli’s journey, I later became a *Flight Angel*, helping escort a rescue pup from Puerto Rico to the mainland U.S. and fostering the pup over the 2025 New Year holiday.
