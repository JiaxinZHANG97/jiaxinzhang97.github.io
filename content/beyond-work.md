## Dog Person

I’ve always loved dogs and see them as family. They remind me to slow down, stay curious, and appreciate the small, beautiful moments in everyday life.

I adopted my dog, Oli, in 2023 through [<u>Toby's Rescue</u>](https://www.tobysrescue.com/). She was found on the streets of Puerto Rico, rescued by local volunteers, and brought to the DMV area, where I was lucky enough to meet her and welcome her into my family. Inspired by Oli’s journey, I later volunteered as a *Flight Angel*, escorting another rescue pup from Puerto Rico to the mainland U.S. and fostering the pup over the 2025 New Year holiday.
